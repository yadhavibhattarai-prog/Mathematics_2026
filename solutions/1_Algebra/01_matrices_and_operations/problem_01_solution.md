
# Exercise 1: Matrix Size and Entries

Given:

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3\\
0 & 4 & 5
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0\\
-2 & 3\\
4 & 1
\end{pmatrix}
$$

## 1. State the sizes of matrices \(A\) and \(B\).

The size of a matrix is determined by its number of rows and columns.

- Matrix \(A\) has 2 rows and 3 columns.

$$
\boxed{A: 2\times3}
$$

- Matrix \(B\) has 3 rows and 2 columns.

$$
\boxed{B: 3\times2}
$$

## 2. Read off the entries \(a_{12}\), \(a_{23}\), \(b_{21}\), and \(b_{32}\).

In matrix notation, \(a_{ij}\) represents the entry in row \(i\) and column \(j\).

### (a) Find \(a_{12}\)

The entry in row 1, column 2 of matrix \(A\) is \(-1\).

$$
\boxed{a_{12}=-1}
$$

### (b) Find \(a_{23}\)

The entry in row 2, column 3 of matrix \(A\) is \(5\).

$$
\boxed{a_{23}=5}
$$

### (c) Find \(b_{21}\)

The entry in row 2, column 1 of matrix \(B\) is \(-2\).

$$
\boxed{b_{21}=-2}
$$

### (d) Find \(b_{32}\)

The entry in row 3, column 2 of matrix \(B\) is \(1\).

$$
\boxed{b_{32}=1}
$$

## 3. Write the second row of \(A\) and the first column of \(B\) as vectors.

### (a) Second row of \(A\)

The second row of matrix \(A\) contains the entries \(0, 4, 5\).

Written as a row vector:

$$
\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}0 & 4 & 5\end{pmatrix}}
$$

### (b) First column of \(B\)

The first column of matrix \(B\) contains the entries \(1, -2, 4\).

Written as a column vector:

$$
\boxed{
\begin{pmatrix}
1\\
-2\\
4
\end{pmatrix}
}
$$

## Final Answers

1. Sizes: \(A=2\times3\), \(B=3\times2\).
2. Entries: \(a_{12}=-1\), \(a_{23}=5\), \(b_{21}=-2\), \(b_{32}=1\).
3. Second row of \(A\): \(\begin{pmatrix}0 & 4 & 5\end{pmatrix}\).

   First column of \(B\): \(\begin{pmatrix}1 & -2 & 4\end{pmatrix}^{T}\).
