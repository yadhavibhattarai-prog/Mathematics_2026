### Exercise 1. Matrix Size and Entries
Given

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 4 & 5 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
-2 & 3 \\
4 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

1. State the sizes of matrices $A$ and $B$.
2. Read off the entries $a_{12}$, $a_{23}$, $b_{21}$, and $b_{32}$.
3. Write the second row of $A$ and the first column of $B$ as vectors.

> **Why this exercise:** builds basic fluency with matrix notation, indices, rows, and columns.

## 1. State the sizes of matrices \(A\) and \(B\).

## Solution

Given

$$
A=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & -1 & 3 \\
0 & 4 & 5
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 0 \\
-2 & 3 \\
4 & 1
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

### 1. Find the sizes of $A$ and $B$

A matrix's size is written as **number of rows × number of columns**.

- $A$ has 2 rows and 3 columns, so its size is $\boxed{2\times3}$.
- $B$ has 3 rows and 2 columns, so its size is $\boxed{3\times2}$.

### 2. Find the specified entries

The subscript in $a_{ij}$ or $b_{ij}$ indicates the entry in row $i$, column $j$.

- $a_{12}$ is in row 1, column 2 of $A$: $\boxed{a_{12}=-1}$.
- $a_{23}$ is in row 2, column 3 of $A$: $\boxed{a_{23}=5}$.
- $b_{21}$ is in row 2, column 1 of $B$: $\boxed{b_{21}=-2}$.
- $b_{32}$ is in row 3, column 2 of $B$: $\boxed{b_{32}=1}$.

### 3. Write the requested row and column as vectors

The second row of $A$ is

$$
\boxed{\begin{pmatrix}0 & 4 & 5\end{pmatrix}}.
$$

The first column of $B$ is

$$
\boxed{
\begin{pmatrix}
1 \\
-2 \\
4
\end{pmatrix}
}.
$$

## Final Answers

1. $A$ is $2\times3$ and $B$ is $3\times2$.
2. $a_{12}=-1$, $a_{23}=5$, $b_{21}=-2$, and $b_{32}=1$.
3. Second row of $A$: $\begin{pmatrix}0 & 4 & 5\end{pmatrix}$; first column of $B$: $\begin{pmatrix}1 & -2 & 4\end{pmatrix}$.
