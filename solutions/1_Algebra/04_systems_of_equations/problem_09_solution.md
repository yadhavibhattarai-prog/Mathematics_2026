# Exercise 9. A System from a Sum Condition

Find numbers $x,y$ satisfying

$$
x+y=10,\qquad 2x+5y=32.
$$

Solve the system and explain geometrically why the solution is unique.

## Solution

Double the first equation to get $2x+2y=20$. Subtract this from the second equation:

$$
3y=12,\qquad y=4.
$$

Then $x=10-4=6$. The solution is $(x,y)=(6,4)$.

Geometrically, each equation describes a line. Their slopes are different ($-1$ and $-2/5$), so the lines are not parallel and intersect at exactly one point, $(6,4)$.
