# Exercise 2. A Line in Space

Given

$$
L:(x,y,z)=(2,-1,3)+t(1,2,-2).
$$

State a point on the line and its direction vector, check whether $P=(4,3,-1)$ lies on $L$, and find the point corresponding to $t=-1$.

## Solution

The line passes through $p=(2,-1,3)$ and has direction vector $v=(1,2,-2)$. To test $P$, solve from the coordinates:

$$
2+t=4\implies t=2,\qquad -1+2t=3\implies t=2.
$$

But the third coordinate would then be $3-2(2)=-1$, matching $P$. Thus $P$ lies on $L$ (at $t=2$). For $t=-1$,

$$
(x,y,z)=(2,-1,3)-(1,2,-2)=(1,-3,5).
$$
