# Exercise 3. Plane Through a Point with a Given Normal Vector

Find the equation of the plane through $P=(1,-2,3)$ and perpendicular to $n=(2,1,-1)$. Check the result by substituting the point $P$ and explain the role of the normal vector.

## Solution

A plane perpendicular to the vector $n$ has $n$ as a normal vector. Using the point-normal equation,

$$
2(x-1)+(y+2)-(z-3)=0,
$$

which simplifies to

$$
2x+y-z+3=0.
$$

Substitution of $P$ gives $2(1)+(-2)-3+3=0$, so the point lies on the plane. The normal vector is perpendicular to every direction lying in the plane and determines its orientation.
