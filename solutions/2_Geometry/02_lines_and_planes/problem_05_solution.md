# Exercise 5. Relative Position of Two Lines

Analyze the position of

$$
L_1:(x,y,z)=(1,1,0)+t(1,2,1),
$$

$$
L_2:(x,y,z)=(2,0,1)+s(0,1,-1).
$$

If they intersect, give the intersection point. If not, determine whether they are parallel or skew. Justify the classification.

## Solution

The direction vectors $(1,2,1)$ and $(0,1,-1)$ are not scalar multiples, so the lines are not parallel. For an intersection, equate coordinates:

$$
1+t=2\implies t=1,\qquad
t=1-s\implies s=0.
$$

But the $y$-coordinates would require $1+2(1)=0+0$, or $3=0$, which is impossible. The lines do not intersect and are not parallel; therefore they are skew.
