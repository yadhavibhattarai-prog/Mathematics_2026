# Exercise 6. Angle Between Planes

Given

$$
\Pi_1:x+y+z=1,\qquad \Pi_2:x-y=2.
$$

First use the normal vectors to decide whether the planes are parallel. If not, compute the angle between them and explain why the normal vectors alone are enough for this calculation.

## Solution

The normal vectors are $n_1=(1,1,1)$ and $n_2=(1,-1,0)$. They are not scalar multiples, so the planes are not parallel. Their dot product is

$$
n_1\cdot n_2=1-1+0=0,
$$

so the normals are perpendicular. The angle between the planes (taken as the acute or right angle between their normal directions) is therefore $90^\circ=\pi/2$. Normals determine the orientations of planes, and the angle between planes is the angle between their normals.
