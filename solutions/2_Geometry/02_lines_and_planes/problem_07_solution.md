# Exercise 7. A Line Relative to a Plane

For

$$
L:(x,y,z)=(1,1,0)+t(1,-1,0),\qquad \Pi:x+y+z=2,
$$

determine whether the line intersects the plane at one point, is parallel to it, or lies in it. Explain the role of the dot product of the direction vector and the normal vector.

## Solution

The plane has normal vector $n=(1,1,1)$, and the line direction is $v=(1,-1,0)$. Since

$$
n\cdot v=1-1+0=0,
$$

the line is parallel to the plane or contained in it. Its base point $(1,1,0)$ lies on the plane because $1+1+0=2$. Hence every point on the line lies in the plane. The zero dot product means the line's direction is parallel to the plane.
